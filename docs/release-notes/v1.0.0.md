**The complete base game**, playable online with 2 to 6 wizards, bots, a lobby and a wins leaderboard.

- All 125 base cards implemented and checked against the scans: 60 spell cards, 25 Treasures, 25 Dead Wizard cards, Wild Magic and 16 heroes
- Turn order announced after spells lock, with the spell name lit as each card goes off, and the dice, damage and healing shown step by step
- Treasures fly to their owner (and between wizards when stolen); revealed cards, deck and discard piles on screen
- Hover any card for its type, glyph, Initiative and roll table
- Reconnecting after a refresh, and a vote to let a bot take over for a dropped player

Not included yet: Expansion 1 (Blood, Standee, Creatures).

To run exactly this version: `.\tools\update-server.ps1 -Image ghcr.io/jrb215/epic-spell-wars-server:1.0.0`
